## Costs and release limits

The [provider's published free plan](https://www.openwebninja.com/api/jsearch) provides 200 request credits per month. Each page of up to 10 results consumes one credit, per the [official API schema](https://github.com/OpenWeb-Ninja/openwebninja-mcp/blob/main/src/generated/manifest.ts). The listed Pro plan is $25/month for 10,000 requests, with additional usage charges. No subscription was purchased or upgraded.

JobRadar continues to enforce 180 requests per rolling 30 days, 20 per UTC day globally and three per account per UTC day. These are application counters, not the provider's authoritative balance. Public employer collection is the base inventory; paid aggregation can supplement coverage once cost per useful listing is measured. The existing scheduled collection/digest workflows remain paused.

Unit, browser and service-role SQL tests cover scope validation, private cursors, duplicate suppression, replay, concurrency, failed requests, expired continuations and caps. Existing Supabase advisories and public-registration setup limits are documented in [the hosted discovery operations guide](../supabase/README.md).
